All protected and conserved areas

Based on our current knowledge, 81 percent of the 675 native vascular plant species known to the peninsula have been reported within its protected and conserved areas, from national park reserve and ecological reserves to regional parks and lands held by conservancies. The other 131 have so far been recorded only outside them.

Key Biodiversity Areas

14 published, 8 of them globally significant · 11 proposed · 781 hectares

Key Biodiversity Areas mark the places that matter most for the persistence of species and ecosystems. The peninsula's Garry oak meadows, rocky headlands and islets hold 14 published Key Biodiversity Areas, most of them identified for their threatened plants, and 11 more are proposed. Together the published KBAs hold 55 percent of the native plant diversity known to the peninsula: 372 of its 675 native vascular plant species have been reported within them.

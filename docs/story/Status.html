Community science contributions

Through the combined efforts of 8154 individuals, the Saanich naturalist community has now documented a total of 1397 vascular plant species. Based on search efforts over the last decade, 100 percent of the historically reported plant diversity has been confirmed and 367 new species reports added to the list. Community members have now documented 59 percent of the plant diversity known to the region on iNaturalist.

The lost plants of the W̱SÁNEĆ/Saanich Peninsula

Can you find these plants? These native species were once recorded on the Saanich Peninsula, but no one has reported them in decades. Some may be lost to us—overlooked, forgotten, or waiting to be rediscovered. Others may be lost because of us, through impacts like habitat change or the introduction of invasive species.

Pick a plant from the gallery to see where and when it was last found, then join local naturalists and community members on a search for the “Lost” Plants of the Saanich Peninsula project on iNaturalist!

Szczawinski and Harrison’s (1972) Flora of the Saanich Peninsula remains a regional classic. Harvey Janszen devoted much of his life to revising this flora, but passed away before completing this work. A community of naturalists has since carried those efforts forward; this atlas is a testament to that continuing legacy.

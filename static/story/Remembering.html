Harvey’s footsteps

The plants of the Saanich Peninsula have been known, named and cared for by many people. The W̱SÁNEĆ people have known them for countless generations, and their relationships with these plants and places continue today.

Since the earliest collection in this atlas, in 1859, many others have added what they noticed: botanists and collectors, professionals and amateurs, neighbours and naturalists still out looking today. Some wrote down the names of plants growing in places. Many more carried what they knew in memory, and though it was never recorded, that knowledge was vital to the stewardship of these lands.

This project is in memory of Harvey Janszen (July 11, 1946–May 10, 2021) and others like Hans Roemer, who have passed away in recent years. Harvey spent decades getting to know the plants of the Salish Sea. He collected over 3,000 vascular plant specimens, kept field journals from 1973 to 2017, and curated species checklists for the southern Gulf Islands, the Saanich Peninsula, the San Juan Islands and beyond.

This project is at once in memory of the plants themselves. Each record is a moment when someone stopped to attend to a plant in its place. Some of those plants are still waiting to be found again. Some may never be.

“Harvey was a brilliant, complex man, and is deeply missed.” — Pam Janszen
